About

Backend depth, applied to AI

I'm an AI engineer and backend lead. For most of my career I've built the systems behind products: REST APIs, microservices, search and the integrations that connect them. Over the last two years I've focused on putting LLMs into those systems in ways that hold up in production.

At Turing I worked on foundational LLM improvement projects for FAAMG partners, designing prompts, multi-turn conversations and function-calling scenarios, and building evaluation frameworks to find where models fail. That work shapes how I build now: retrieval first, outputs evaluated, failure modes planned for.

At DevDen I lead backend work for Nice2Stay, a global booking platform, where Elasticsearch powers retrieval over a large property dataset.
